Transcription Guidelines Followed


unintelligible word: X (one X per syllable)

in (brackets) when unsure of a word or phrase

in <arrows> when the examiner or third party is speaking

filler non-words (uh, eh, uhm) not transcribed, but filler words such as zo (so), en (and), nou (well) are transcribed

incomplete words indicated by a hyphen (hyph-)
